1. What this extension is

Amatti Helper is an independent, unofficial extension that adds printing and follow-up tools (group lists, certificates, statistics, staff lists and a subject-assignment assistant) to the Amatti school-management platform at amatti.education.dz, for users who are signed in to that platform. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or published by the Algerian Ministry of National Education.

2. Information the extension handles

While you use the platform, its pages display information such as student names, dates of birth, registration numbers, guardians' names and phone numbers, staff details and subject assignments. The extension reads this information inside your browser only, in order to prepare the documents you ask for and to run the assignment actions you explicitly request.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not collect your data or the platform's data, and we do not transmit it to any server owned or operated by the developer.
  • We do not sell, rent, share or transfer data to third parties.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, tracking pixels or fingerprinting.
  • We do not read, store or transmit passwords. Requests to the platform rely on your existing signed-in session.
  • We do not load or execute remote code; all code, including the open-source pdf-lib library, is bundled in the extension.

4. Network activity

The extension contacts only https://amatti.education.dz/, using the same endpoints the platform's own pages use (for example student lists, certificates, statistics and the assignment list). Printing and PDF merging are done locally in your browser.

5. Local storage

A few settings are saved on your device with chrome.storage.local. They are never sent to the developer or any third party and are removed when you uninstall the extension.

6. Permissions

  • storage – to save the local settings described above.
  • Access to https://amatti.education.dz/* – to run the tools inside the platform's pages, read what the platform shows you, and request data or certificates with your session.

7. Limited Use

The use of information received through the extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: data is used only to provide the user-facing features described above, is not transferred to others except as necessary to provide those features (requests to the platform), is not used for advertising, and is not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

8. Retention and deletion

The developer does not hold any of your data. Local settings remain on your device until you clear the extension's data or uninstall it. Documents you print or save are under your control and your institution's responsibility.

9. Security

Data is processed locally in your browser, and communication with the platform uses HTTPS. You should handle printed lists and certificates in line with the rules that apply to student and staff data in your institution.

10. Children's information

The platform contains information about students, who are minors. The extension does not collect or store this information outside your device and does not send it anywhere other than the platform itself.

11. Changes to this policy

If the extension's behavior changes, this policy will be updated and the date above will change.

1. ما هي الإضافة

إضافة مستقلة وغير رسمية تضيف أدوات طباعة ومتابعة (قوائم الأفواج، الشهادات، الإحصائيات، قوائم الموظفين ومساعد إسناد المواد) إلى منصة أمتي (amatti.education.dz) لمن سجّل دخوله فيها. وهي ليست تابعة لوزارة التربية الوطنية ولا صادرة عنها.

2. البيانات التي تعالجها الإضافة

تعرض المنصة بيانات مثل أسماء التلاميذ وتواريخ ميلادهم وأرقام تسجيلهم وأسماء الأولياء وهواتفهم وبيانات الموظفين وإسناد المواد. تقرأ الإضافة هذه البيانات داخل متصفحك فقط لتحضير المستندات التي تطلبها ولتنفيذ عمليات الإسناد التي تطلبها صراحة. والنصوص الاختيارية التي تكتبها في حقول الشهادة تُرسل إلى المنصة وحدها لإنشاء الشهادة.

3. ما لا نفعله

  • لا نجمع بياناتك ولا بيانات المنصة، ولا نرسلها إلى أي خادم يملكه المطوّر.
  • لا نبيع البيانات ولا نشاركها مع أي جهة ثالثة.
  • لا نستعمل إعلانات ولا تتبّعاً ولا تحليلات.
  • لا نقرأ كلمات المرور ولا نخزّنها؛ تعتمد الطلبات على جلسة تسجيل دخولك.
  • لا نحمّل ولا ننفّذ كوداً بعيداً؛ كل الشيفرة مضمّنة في الإضافة.

4. الاتصال بالشبكة

لا تتصل الإضافة إلا بـ https://amatti.education.dz/ وبنقاط الاتصال نفسها التي تستعملها صفحات المنصة. وتتم الطباعة ودمج ملفات PDF محلياً في متصفحك.

5. التخزين المحلي

تُحفظ إعدادات بسيطة (اسم المديرية والمؤسسة وتفضيلات ربط أعمدة الجداول) على جهازك عبر chrome.storage.local، ولا تُرسل إلى أي جهة، وتُحذف عند إزالة الإضافة.
